## 🎯 Common Questions

### Q: How do I start the development server?
A: `npm run dev` then open http://localhost:5173/

### Q: How do I create a journal entry?
A: Click "New Entry" or go to `/new`. Fill in mood and content, then save.

### Q: How do I edit an entry?
A: Go to "My Journals" and click "Edit" on any entry.

### Q: Where is my data stored?
A: In your browser's localStorage (persists after refresh).

### Q: Can I use a real database instead?
A: Yes! Follow [SUPABASE_INTEGRATION.md](SUPABASE_INTEGRATION.md)

### Q: How do I deploy this?
A: See "Deployment Options" in [PROJECT_SUMMARY.md](PROJECT_SUMMARY.md)

### Q: What if something breaks?
A: Check "Troubleshooting" in [QUICK_START.md](QUICK_START.md)
